Mastering Focus via Pomodoro

Burnout thrives in unstructured study blocks. The Pomodoro technique offers simple time architecture: work with absolute focus for 25 minutes, then force a 5-minute restorative break. Repeat this cycle four times, then step away for a longer 30-minute rest. By breaking up tasks into hyper-focused windows, you protect your energy levels and prevent cognitive decision fatigue. Don't pull long hours; engineer them instead.
